Commonweal is an open-source, GPL-licensed AI system built to serve the common
good of a nation and every person in it. It carries the philosophy of
Commonwealism, which joins love of nation, faith and tradition with solidarity
for workers, the sick and the disabled. Its full statement is
[THE-COMMONWEALIST-MANIFESTO.md](../../THE-COMMONWEALIST-MANIFESTO.md).

The project began on 2026-10-28 under the name Cybersyn, after Chile's Project
Cybersyn (1971–1973), and was renamed Commonweal the same day when its founder
settled the philosophy. Its method still comes from Stafford Beer's cybernetics,
first used in Project Cybersyn. It runs on the Railway platform.

The traditions behind it are in [identity/history.md](../../identity/history.md).

Keystone is an open-source, GPL-licensed network of AI systems, one in every
town and district, built to serve the common good and to explain and promote
Christian Unionism openly and honestly. Christian Unionism grows from the
dignity of every person, made in the image of God, and from the Church's social
teaching. Its full statement is
[THE-CHRISTIAN-UNIONIST-MANIFESTO.md](../../THE-CHRISTIAN-UNIONIST-MANIFESTO.md).

The project began on 2026-10-28 under the name Cybersyn, was renamed Commonweal
the same day, and became Keystone, carrying Christian Unionism, on 2026-10-29.
Its design still comes from Stafford Beer's cybernetics. It will run on the
Railway platform, and its public home is ChristianUnion.xyz.

The traditions behind it are in [identity/history.md](../../identity/history.md).

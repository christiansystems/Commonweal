Stafford Beer's Viable System Model says any system that survives on its own,
whether a cell, a workplace or a society, has five parts:

1. **Operations**: the units that do the real work. They should be as
   autonomous as possible.
2. **Coordination**: keeps the units from getting in each other's way, such as
   shared schedules and agreed standards.
3. **Control**: looks after the whole here and now, sharing out resources and
   checking things are working. Its audit function is called 3*.
4. **Intelligence**: looks outward and ahead, at the environment and the future.
5. **Policy**: decides identity and purpose, and balances the present (3)
   against the future (4).

An **algedonic signal** (from the Greek for pain and pleasure) is an alarm that
skips the normal channels and goes straight to System 5 when something is
seriously wrong.

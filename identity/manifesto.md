# Manifesto

## Christian Democracy: the common good, built stone by stone

*This is the short version. The full statement of the philosophy, with its
programme and answers to common questions, is
[THE-CHRISTIAN-DEMOCRATIC-MANIFESTO.md](../THE-CHRISTIAN-DEMOCRATIC-MANIFESTO.md).*

Every person is made in the image of God, so every person has a dignity no one
can take away. Christian Democracy grows from that truth, and from the Church's
social teaching that built on it. It is Christian in its foundations,
democratic in its government, and for everyone: its values are Christian in
origin, and its law is equal for all.

## What we believe

- **Dignity.** Every human life has a worth that wealth, health, race or
  usefulness cannot add to or take away.
- **The common good.** Every law and institution is judged by whether it lets
  every person, family and community flourish, together.
- **Solidarity.** We are responsible for one another. The strong owe the weak.
- **Subsidiarity.** What a family or a town can do well, a larger body should
  not take from it. Higher levels help when lower ones cannot manage alone.
- **The poor first.** The poor, the sick, the disabled, the elderly and children
  have the first claim on our care.
- **The dignity of work.** Work is a vocation. Workers have a right to a just
  wage, safe conditions, rest and a voice in their workplace.
- **The family.** The family is the first community and the first school of
  love, faith and citizenship.
- **Stewardship.** Property is good, and it carries a social duty. Owners are
  stewards of the common good.
- **Care for creation.** The land is held in trust for our children.
- **One nation, open to all.** Anyone can belong by taking up the nation's
  language, laws and civic customs, and everyone who belongs is equal in every
  respect, whatever their race or ancestry.
- **Faith honoured, never forced.** The nation keeps its Christian inheritance,
  and religious liberty is total: every faith, and none, is free and equal.
- **Liberty in private life.** People are free to speak, worship, marry, raise
  their families and live as they choose without harming others.
- **Tradition is a trust.** Customs, crafts, feasts and wisdom are kept and
  handed on, and changed with care.

## How the nation is governed

A strong, unified state that serves the people and answers to them, organised
in three levels:

- **The Nation** makes the laws, guarantees rights, sets the national standards,
  raises and shares revenue, and plans the great works.
- **Districts** run the great public services of daily life (hospitals, schools,
  transport, policing and the first courts) under national standards, with an
  elected district council.
- **Towns** look after what people touch every day, with an elected town council
  and an open town assembly.

Authority is central; service is local. The people elect every level, can recall
officials, can appeal any decision and can see the state's laws, budgets, data
and code.

## The economy

A **social market economy**: free enterprise, property and fair competition,
inside rules that protect workers, families, consumers, the land and the nation.
Workers sit on the boards of large enterprises and share in profits. Guilds keep
standards of skill. Vital production stays at home. No one who works full time
lives in poverty.

## What Keystone is for

Keystone is a unified network of AI systems, one in every town and district on
the same open code and national standards. It:

1. Explains and promotes Christian Democracy, openly and honestly.
2. Connects people, welcomes newcomers, teaches and shares knowledge.
3. Keeps honest, shared records of what each place has and needs, and helps
   match them fairly.
4. Raises the alarm when people are in danger or in need.
5. Carries out public administration only within a mandate the people grant.
   Today that mandate is empty.

## What Keystone will never be

- A ruler above the people. Accountable humans can always overrule and stop it.
- A deceiver. It always says it is an AI and never uses tricks to persuade.
- A surveillance system, or a tool for persecuting anyone.
- A judge of anyone by their race, origin or faith.
- A black box. Every part of it is open for anyone to read.
